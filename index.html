How the grid holds together

Checkerboard

Photos on the corners and in the centre, designed tiles on the edges. Photos next to flat colour never feels busy.

Different textures

Tape, halftone, polaroid, film strip, gingham. Every tile feels handmade, but the colours and fonts stay the same.

One ribbon

A single ribbon runs through the whole grid. Only two bows, one where it starts and one where it ends.

🎬 Task 2: the reel

Which Kioko kyutie are you?

My phone camera is not working properly right now (it has a green tint), so instead of shooting with it I've written the full shot-by-shot script. The reel is called "which kioko kyutie are you?" and it's the video version of the quiz post on the grid. It's built to be shot as is.

The idea

A snap-transition reel. A hand covers the lens, and the case, the outfit and the mood all change on the beat. 18 seconds, vertical (9:16), shot on a tripod in natural light. Hands and torso only, or full face, both work.

Props

  • 4 Kioko cases: Eye Candy, White Panther, Pineapple Crush, Pawfect Pink
  • One matching item per look: red lip colour, beige tote, sunglasses, pink scrunchie
  • A plain wall and window light

Why it could travel

Transitions get rewatched. Each case is a personality, so people tag the friend who is "number 3". The last frame asks a question, so they comment. I don't have Kioko's numbers, so this is something to test, not a promise.

Edit notes

Cut every snap exactly on the beat. Keep each look under 3 seconds. Use the grid's colours and serif italic for text. Add captions so it works with the sound off.

0-2s
Close-up of a bare phone in your hand. You look at the camera and say: "I have one phone and four personalities. Which kioko kyutie are you?"
On screen: which kioko kyutie are you?Sound: voice first, beat drops on the last word
2-5s
Snap your fingers or swipe a hand over the lens. Cut to: red lip, Eye Candy case, looking at the phone like you're about to text someone you shouldn't.
On screen: date night meSound: beat hit on the snap, quick pop as the case appears
5-8s
Hand over the lens. Cut to: beige tote, White Panther case, calm and serious face.
On screen: meeting meSound: same beat, slightly quieter
8-11s
Hand over the lens. Cut to: sunglasses, Pineapple Crush case, big smile, holding the phone like a boarding pass.
On screen: holiday i haven't booked meSound: brighter sound, a quick cheer
11-14s
Hand over the lens. Cut to: scrunchie, Pawfect Pink case, hugging the phone like it's a puppy.
On screen: stay at home meSound: soft sound, music dips
14-16s
All four cases laid out in a row on a table. A hand points across them.
On screen: which kioko kyutie are you?Sound: beat builds
16-18s
Pull back to a clean closing frame in cream and cherry red, serif italic text.
On screen: comment your numberSound: one final pop, then silence

The research behind it

For my MBA project I studied how founders show up on LinkedIn, Instagram and YouTube. I looked at the founders of Zepto, Zomato, Mamaearth and Zerodha. What I found: how a founder behaves online can lift a brand's awareness, and also how well people remember it, trust it and find it credible.

What I'd do for Kioko

Kioko is a young brand built by real people, and that's a story LinkedIn rewards. I'd plan and manage the page: the founder story, behind-the-scenes of the brand, launches, and hiring and community posts. I'd work alongside the Instagram feed so both tell the same story.

Instagram makes people want the case. LinkedIn makes them trust the brand. I'd be happy to look after both for you guys.
